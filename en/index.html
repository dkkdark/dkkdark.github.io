Support

For help with Cloud Sheep, email info.xenonimus@gmail.com. Include the game version, device model, pack and island number, and what went wrong. Remove personal information from screenshots.

Settings → Support prepares an email with a technical summary. You can edit it before sending; nothing is sent automatically. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact us.

Privacy policy

Your game, your device

Your progress stays on your device. No account is required. Internet connections are used to check hint refill times, for optional Google advertisements, and when you open support links. There is no developer-operated analytics or automatic crash-reporting service.

Who is responsible?

Kseniia Blokhina is responsible for the processing described as ours in this notice. Contact: info.xenonimus@gmail.com. This notice covers the Android game Cloud Sheep, this website and support correspondence.

What data is used, and why?

  • Playing: progress, the current attempt, move history, treasures, coins, skins, hints, reward records and settings are stored locally so you can continue playing. Profile and wallet records use protected platform storage. We do not receive these saves on a server. Real-money purchases, account sign-in and cloud synchronisation are not enabled in this version.
  • Hint refill time: the app reads the server date through HTTPS at www.google.com/generate_204. Google receives your IP address and connection details. A random request token avoids cached responses; no name, saved game, coin balance or persistent player identifier is sent. This prevents changes to the device clock from granting extra hints. A failed connection can delay a refill.
  • Optional advertising: Google AdMob starts only when you request a rewarded video. It may process IP address (including approximate location derived from it), advertising interactions, technical diagnostics and SDK identifiers, as described in Google's SDK disclosure. Reward records stay on the device. There are no automatic interstitials or banners, and restarting a level is free. Developer mode disables ads; development builds use sample ads, which also connect to Google.
  • Age and ad choices: before the first real ad, you may select a broad age group or choose not to say. Only that selection is saved locally, not a birth date. Children, all other under-18s and unknown ages receive child-restricted requests with the general-audience content rating. No personalised advertising is requested for any age, and Android advertising-ID permission is removed. Google receives the applicable restriction signal, not your selected age band. Google UMP handles any required advertising consent for adults; children are not asked to give adult advertising consent. Where consent is required and unavailable, no ad is requested. Adult consent choices can be reopened in Settings → Ad privacy.
  • Support: when you email us, we receive your address, sender name, message and chosen attachments. The optional technical summary contains app/build version, device model, operating system and game situation. It excludes passwords, advertising identifiers, receipts and the full save. We use it to answer and investigate your enquiry; our mailbox uses Gmail.
  • This website: GitHub Pages receives IP addresses and technical request information for hosting and security. We add no cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, contact forms or external fonts. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The game does not need contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. Sheep sounds are prerecorded. Support is optional, and local play does not require a name or email address. Without a reply address, we cannot answer an enquiry.

How long is data kept?

Local game and age-choice records remain until you erase the relevant app data. Operating-system backups may retain copies according to your settings. Support messages are kept while an enquiry is open and for up to six months after closure, unless a legal retention obligation or a specific dispute requires longer. Google and GitHub set retention for their own records according to the purposes and obligations in their policies; we do not hold copies of their connection logs.

Deletion and your rights

For access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or an objection to legitimate-interest processing, email info.xenonimus@gmail.com. Parents may contact us about their child's data. You may withdraw consent for the future without affecting earlier lawful processing. We normally respond to GDPR requests within one month and explain any lawful extension.

Reset level progress in the game resets levels and tutorials, keeping treasures, skins and coins. We cannot remotely delete a device-only save. Android's Settings → Apps → Cloud Sheep → Storage → Clear data removes local game data; device wording may differ. Manage backups separately. This may permanently remove your collected content. Email us for help or deletion of support correspondence. There is no developer-hosted game account.

You can complain to a data protection authority, including where you live or work. German authorities are listed by the Datenschutzkonferenz. We do not make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. This page is updated when processing changes; any new consent is requested separately.
